Perf gate, 2026-09-12, tablet on mainline 7.3.0-rc2 (build 20260908), QLC+
5.2.2 with Vibra.qxw on the Mac at 192.168.1.65:9998, DMXDESK_PERF=1
DMXDESK_DRAG=1 (the fader's requested level moves every loop and the tile
repaints, sending nothing: the load of a finger dragging the master).

Each line is the desk's own ten-second report: p50/p95 of the last thousand
paint and present times in ms, the sample count, RSS and MemAvailable.

1. Full repaint every frame, per-pixel rounded rectangles, the status bar
   supersampled on every frame:
   perf: paint p50 217 p95 248 ms (161) present p50 20 p95 25 ms (161) rss 7844 kB memavail 890284 kB

2. Rounded rectangles as row spans, the status bar painted once per change
   and copied:
   perf: paint p50 41 p95 81 ms (497) present p50 14 p95 23 ms (497) rss 8040 kB memavail 892900 kB

3. Damage per control, every primitive clipped to it, the presenter copying
   only the union of this frame's and the other buffer's pending damage
   (the background clear still unclipped):
   perf: paint p50 31 p95 63 ms (738) present p50 5 p95 16 ms (738) rss 8044 kB memavail 892956 kB

4. The background clear clipped as well:
   perf: paint p50 14 p95 16 ms (1557) present p50 4 p95 5 ms (1557) rss 7976 kB memavail 892968 kB

Verdict: under the 33 ms p95 budget of the design with margin; a full-page
repaint (a page or bank change) still costs about 41 ms once, which is one
frame at the panel's rate and is not on the drag path. No GLES presentation
needed.
